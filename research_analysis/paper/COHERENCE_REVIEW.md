# Coherence Review

## Narrative strength

The coherent thread is “prediction and measurement are not semantic identification.” The strongest version moves from pair-prior baselines, to the operational WPRD target, to geometry visibility, then to the nested fixed-parent predictive result and its mixed predicate effects. The ending can state a bounded predictive result and a strict semantic boundary without trying to resolve the unavailable R1 endpoint.

## Redundant sections

- The current draft repeats the semantic caveat in the introduction, scope, results, discussion, limitations, and conclusion. Retain it at the framing point, after R2, and in conclusion; use precise section-specific limitations elsewhere.
- WPRD correlations are repeated in abstract, methods, results, and discussion. Keep the coefficient pair in results/supplement and a short dependence caveat in abstract or discussion, not both at length.
- The R1 interruption is repeated in geometry methods/results/discussion. Explain the defect once in results; refer to the unavailable endpoint in limitations.
- The historical ladder currently carries too much weight relative to its non-matched protocols.

## Missing transition

Explain why a within-pair metric can remove pair-constant score variation yet still remain sensitive to geometry, appearance, and context. That bridge is essential between the prior controls and WPRD's narrow validity boundary.

## Strongest section

The corrected nested R2 methods/results are strongest because parent, offset, fit/check populations, nesting, point estimate, and uncertainty are explicitly recorded.

## Weakest section

WPRD construct validity is weakest for broad inference: n=12 dependent arms, incomplete test-cell records, and absent planted-shortcut/VLM controls. It can be diagnostic, not the paper's broad validation centerpiece.

## Recommended reordering

1. Introduce the distinction between prediction and semantic interpretation.
2. Present pair-prior and readout evidence as motivation, with historical population labels.
3. Define WPRD and show its limited pair-prior control; immediately state what it cannot rule out.
4. Present the geometry input-contract audit and explicitly separate construction from performance effect.
5. Present corrected nested R2 early and in detail, since it is the clearest current predictive contrast.
6. Follow with predicate-level heterogeneity and the accuracy/macro-recall divergence.
7. Put dependent-arm correlations and the N4-FIT pilot in a bounded supplementary/limitations context.
8. Conclude with the constrained measurement/predictive claim, not a semantic conclusion.
