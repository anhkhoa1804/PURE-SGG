# Paper C closeout

**Disposition: `PAPER_C_DIRECTION_CLOSED`.** The current hypothesis branch is
closed; there is no authorized next experiment in this repository state.

The evidence began with decodability and representation/readout comparisons,
then progressively stronger nuisance controls. Predictive utility relative to
an ordered-pair prior was substantial, but the increment after geometry was
small and uncertain. A leakage-safe bounded N4-FIT pilot still showed a small
CAL-CHECK improvement, but the registered resource rule required an effect
materially larger than the prior G+O residual reference; that condition was
not met. Fullscale U/S replay was deliberately not pursued.

The direction was stopped to avoid treating a small pilot-scale predictive
residual as evidence for a semantic mechanism or as sufficient justification
for an expensive full-population replay. This stop does not demonstrate
equivalence, absence of residual information, or closure of the fullscale
nuisance gate. It means the bounded pilot did not justify the resource cost
under the frozen rule.

Deliberately not pursued from this branch: full 83,249-image N4-FIT U/S
reconstruction, residual-supervised C1 training, seed-5678 smoke replication,
B2 inference intervention, and an interactional-family endpoint. None is
authorized by this closeout.

The current evidence does not establish relational semantics, causal
information, open-vocabulary relation understanding, interactional-family
superiority, or residual beyond full-population G+O+U+S. Current validation
contains zero rows eligible under the frozen interactional-family definition.

A future project should state a new research hypothesis, identify its target
estimand and nuisance alternatives, and register a bounded protocol before
reusing these artifacts. That would be a new direction, not continuation of
an unbounded diagnostic loop.
